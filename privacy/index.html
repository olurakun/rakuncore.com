Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This policy describes how the photo, video and voice recording backup app developed by Rakun Core (the Android app and the Windows/macOS desktop app) handles your data.

In short

The app does not send any personal data to us or to third parties. It uses no accounts, cloud servers, advertising, analytics or crash reporting services. Your files travel only between your phone and the computer you paired it with.

Data processed

  • Photos, videos and voice recordings: read from your phone and sent to the computer you paired, to back them up. Files are transferred unchanged, including location data, so the backup is identical to the original.
  • File details: name, size, date and SHA‑256 fingerprint, kept on the phone and the computer to verify which files are backed up.
  • Pairing details: the computer's name, local network address and connection key, and the phone's model name, kept on the devices so they recognise each other.

None of this is sent over the internet. The app only allows connections to local network addresses.

Permissions

  • Access to photos, videos and audio: for backing them up.
  • Access to photo locations: without it Android hands apps a copy with location data removed, and the backup would not match the original. The app does not use or send your location.
  • Camera: only while scanning the pairing QR code.
  • Media management (optional): to move verified files to the trash without asking each time.
  • Network access: only to connect to your computer on the local network.

Security

The connection between phone and computer is encrypted, and the phone only trusts the computer it was paired with by QR code. With the optional encrypted backup, files are encrypted on the phone with your password; the password and recovery key are never sent in the clear to us or to the computer.

Deleting your data

Your data exists only on your own devices. Uninstalling the app removes its data from the phone; you can delete the backups on your computer at any time. We hold no data about you to delete.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from children; it collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the current version is published on this page with a new date.
